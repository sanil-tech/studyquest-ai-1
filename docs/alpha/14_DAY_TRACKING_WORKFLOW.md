# 14-Day Pilot Tracking Workflow

This is the daily operational schedule for the founding team during the Alpha test.

## Day 1: Onboarding Verification
- Ensure all 5 families successfully log in.
- Monitor telemetry for immediate failure points during the Diagnostic Assessment.
- **Goal**: 100% login rate.

## Days 2-6: Monitoring Phase
- Run the Daily KPI Snapshot every morning at 09:00 MYT.
- Watch the `AlphaUserTracker` for "Struggling" or "At Risk" tags.
- Triage any incoming bugs according to the `INCIDENT_REVIEW_PROCESS`.

## Day 7: Mid-Point Check
- Send out a short, automated qualitative survey to the Parents.
- **Focus**: "Is the app easy to use? Is your child enjoying it?"

## Days 8-13: Deep Analytics Phase
- Analyze the AI Tutor Recovery Rate. Is Cikgu AI actually helping them pass widgets, or are they getting stuck?
- Monitor the Mastery Gain. Are students actually progressing through the Taxonomy?

## Day 14: Final Assessment & Offboarding
- Trigger a final assessment module to measure competency delta from Day 1.
- Send the final qualitative Parent Survey (Focus: "Would you pay for this?").
- Revoke Alpha access and prepare for Beta / Batch 002 curriculum generation.
